Carry Trade: borrowing low, investing high

How to read
Carry build-up
Borrow the low-yield currency (blue) and buy the high-yield currency (orange). As risk appetite rises, traders push the high-yield currency higher.

Risk-on phase: Steady upward slope for the carry target while funding stays flat or softens.

Unwind: A sharp downward step as positions are closed and capital rushes back to the funding currency.
